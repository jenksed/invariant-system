# Core Concepts

Invariant is easier to understand as a set of separations than as a list of products.

- [Doctrine](doctrine.md) — design rules that recur across the system.
- [Authority](authority.md) — who may propose, select, execute, mutate, verify, and accept.
- [Evidence](evidence.md) — why completion must be bound to observable state.
- [Completion](completion.md) — why proposed/applied/executed/verified/accepted are different states.
- [Context](context.md) — why working context is compiled rather than accumulated indefinitely.
- [Qualification](qualification.md) — how Bench evidence differs from Manifold selection and Kiln authority.
- [Glossary](glossary.md) — the internal nouns after the ideas are clear.
