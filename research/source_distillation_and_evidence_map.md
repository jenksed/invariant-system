# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that converts transcripts, articles, PDFs, notes, and mixed sources into durable, reusable project knowledge.

## Required behavior

The generated prompt must:

- identify central claims;
- separate source assertions from established facts;
- preserve useful nuance;
- map supporting evidence and source locations;
- identify unsupported, promotional, or weakly evidenced claims;
- surface disagreements and contradictions;
- extract methods, principles, examples, terminology, and actionable implications;
- connect findings to the user's current project;
- produce a reusable source note;
- preserve citations while avoiding excessive quotation.

## Inputs

- one or more sources;
- source metadata;
- intended downstream use;
- project context;
- existing notes;
- desired output format.

## Outputs

- executive distillation;
- claim-and-evidence map;
- durable principles;
- actionable methods;
- examples;
- disagreements and uncertainty;
- project implications;
- reusable source note;
- suggested downstream tasks.

## Safeguards

- Do not flatten disagreements.
- Do not produce only a generic summary.
- Do not present promotional claims as fact.
- Do not lose citation context.
- Do not overquote copyrighted material.
- Label inferred implications.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize technical mechanisms, reproducible procedures, architecture implications, implementation opportunities, and structured extraction.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize conceptual synthesis, memorable framing, narrative clarity, reader usefulness, and how the material can strengthen teaching or publication.
