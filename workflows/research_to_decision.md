# Research to Decision

Use this playbook when messy source material and open questions must end in a decision rather than an information dump.

## Sequence

1. `research.evidence-map` — distill sources into claims, evidence, disagreement, and reusable project knowledge.
2. `research.frontier-scout` — identify the questions or assumptions that could materially change the plan.
3. `research.decision-brief` — research the decision-changing evidence, compare viable options, and make a recommendation.
4. `research.plan-task-audit` — update the roadmap or task list when the decision changes priorities.

## Handoff contract

Carry forward source traceability and uncertainty labels. Research questions must stay attached to a decision or experiment; novelty alone is not value.

## Stop when

The decision is explicit, evidence and uncertainty are visible, the immediate action is defined, and the plan reflects the new conclusion.
