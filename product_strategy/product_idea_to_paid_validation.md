# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that turns a product idea into the smallest honest experiment that tests whether real users care enough to commit, participate, or pay.

## Required behavior

The generated prompt must:

- identify the user and painful situation;
- define the promised outcome;
- identify the riskiest assumption;
- distinguish demand risk from implementation risk;
- design the smallest useful offer;
- define a manual or agent-assisted fulfillment path;
- define price or commitment tests;
- identify a realistic recruiting channel;
- establish success, failure, and ambiguous thresholds;
- specify evidence to capture;
- define what should not be automated before validation;
- end with a short execution plan.

## Inputs

- product idea;
- target users;
- known problem;
- proposed value;
- constraints;
- budget;
- available channels;
- existing prototype or audience;
- desired test period.

## Outputs

- assumption map;
- riskiest assumption;
- testable offer;
- experiment design;
- recruiting message;
- fulfillment workflow;
- pricing or commitment test;
- success and failure criteria;
- evidence plan;
- next decision rules.

## Safeguards

- Do not recommend building a full product before testing demand.
- Do not use survey enthusiasm as proof of willingness to pay.
- Do not automate an unvalidated workflow.
- Do not hide weak results behind vanity metrics.
- Do not design an experiment that cannot produce a decision.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize experiment instrumentation, manual operations, lightweight implementation, payments or commitment evidence, data capture, and decision thresholds.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize user clarity, offer positioning, onboarding experience, trust, messaging, perceived value, and whether the experiment feels credible rather than improvised.
