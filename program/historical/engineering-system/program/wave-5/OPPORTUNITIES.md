## Arsenal

The genuine lift came from composing three deterministic research techniques:
complete topology, bounded structured-manifest parsing, and literal reference
graphing. The benchmark should retain ablation as a graduation requirement;
otherwise a large combined method can win without explaining why.

The productized adapter proof was essential. A research winner alone did not
prove that the adopted TypeScript implementation preserved the behavior.

## Loadout

Swapping the method beneath a stable Capability felt natural: Goal and
Capability remained unchanged, while Plan provenance exposed the new method.
The clean-worktree failure showed that repository-state observation needs to
treat ordinary Git layouts—not only `.git/` directories—as a first-class
product input.

The evidence graph is valuable but extremely large: 88 claims on Temper,
thousands on the other repositories, and 9,446 on the post-Wave-5 Arsenal
checkout. Complete recursive JSON scalar emission dominates output volume.
Future work should investigate evidence indexing, importance tiers, or bounded
detail without weakening truth or benchmark coverage.

## Temper

The operator can see the improved method through Plan provenance, but useful
method evidence is buried in the raw Plan. Opening Plan for the Arsenal
dogfood produced millions of terminal characters. Temper naturally prompts:

- What facts are new relative to the prior Run?
- Which observations mattered to acceptance or method selection?
- Which claims are absences, relationships, or useful unknowns?
- What changed in evidence without changing repository HEAD?

The operator repeatedly needs shell/JQ or benchmark documents to answer those
questions. A before/after Run comparison is now evidence-backed as a candidate,
but it is not automatically the next Wave.

evaluated product target and adapter. The contract question is narrow and
observed. It should be adjudicated separately; an adapter name in notes is not
canonical qualification.
