# Learning to Portfolio

Use this playbook to turn a technical learning goal into demonstrated capability and honest portfolio evidence.

## Sequence

1. `learning.socratic-tutor` — stabilize the core mental model through prediction, correction, and transfer.
2. `learning.session-designer` — structure a focused practical session around one observable capability.
3. `learning.study-guide-builder` — preserve corrected misunderstandings, mental models, recall questions, and transfer exercises.
4. `learning.portfolio-lab-architect` — turn the capability into a reproducible troubleshooting or implementation lab with receipts.

## Handoff contract

Do not promote tutorial completion into mastery. Each stage should preserve what was actually demonstrated, what remains uncertain, and the evidence required by the next stage.

## Stop when

The learner can apply the concept in a new scenario and the resulting lab documents verifiable capability without representing lab work as paid production experience.
