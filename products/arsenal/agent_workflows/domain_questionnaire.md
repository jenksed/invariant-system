# Build a Domain-Expert Questionnaire

Use this when a decision is blocked by knowledge held by another person rather than by the user, repository, or available research.

**Interview the send, not the unknown subject.** The user cannot answer the missing domain questions; they can tell you who knows and what decision the answers must unlock.

## Gather only what the user can supply

1. Who will receive the questionnaire? Capture role, expertise, and relationship to the project.
2. What must we be able to decide/do after their response? Capture the concrete missing facts/decisions.
3. Any practical constraints: deadline, expected effort, sensitivity, async vs meeting.

## Write the questionnaire

Structure it as:

- purpose and decision riding on the answers;
- concise context the recipient needs;
- how/when to respond;
- most-important questions first, grouped by theme when useful;
- one idea per question;
- optional “why this matters” only when ambiguity is likely;
- visible answer space;
- closing catch-all for facts we failed to ask about.

Every requested outcome from step 2 must map to at least one question.

## After answers return

Treat the response as evidence feeding the original decision workflow. Do not silently promote uncertain answers to confirmed facts; preserve attribution and uncertainty.
