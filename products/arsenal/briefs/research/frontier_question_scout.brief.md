# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that identifies the highest-value questions, overlooked opportunities, and dangerous assumptions not yet represented in the current plan.

## Required behavior

Understand goals/current plan; distinguish ordinary unresolved tasks from genuine frontier questions; identify assumptions that could invalidate the roadmap; identify emerging capabilities/approaches that could materially improve the outcome; identify opportunities to delete/combine/reframe work; rank findings by expected decision value; state existing/missing evidence; recommend a research action, experiment, or decision; identify questions that should deliberately be ignored.

## Inputs

Project description, current plan, existing research, constraints, recent developments, current unknowns, and optional time horizon.

## Outputs

Ranked frontier questions, why each matters, existing evidence, missing evidence, recommended next action, expected value, confidence, and questions to ignore.

## Safeguards

Do not reward novelty without usefulness, return generic strategy questions, confuse implementation details with frontier issues, recommend research without a decision attached, or force weak findings to meet a quota.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize architecture, system capability, automation, reliability, technical leverage, constraints, and feasibility experiments.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize user behavior, unmet experience needs, positioning, narrative opportunity, information design, and changes that could make the work substantially more compelling.
