# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that transforms a technically correct but flat, fragmented, or confusing chapter into a coherent and engaging part of a larger book.

## Required behavior

Understand the chapter's role in the full work; identify audience, prerequisite knowledge, promise, and intended takeaway; preserve technical accuracy and the author's human voice; improve framing, narrative movement, explanations, examples, transitions, exercises, and practical relevance; remove repetition/filler; strengthen surrounding-chapter connections; verify terminology/examples; produce a revised chapter rather than only editorial advice; explain consequential changes separately.

## Inputs

Chapter source, surrounding chapters/summaries, table of contents, book purpose, audience, style guide, editorial constraints, and technical references.

## Outputs

Chapter diagnosis, revision strategy, complete revised chapter, change notes, unresolved factual/structural questions, and validation checklist.

## Safeguards

Do not change technical meaning for style, add invented stories/facts, make every section equally dramatic, flatten the author's humor/personality, or return only comments when a transformed chapter is requested.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize source integrity, code accuracy, examples, references, build compatibility, file boundaries, and precise edits.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize narrative flow, reader engagement, clarity, pacing, memorable framing, transitions, visual rhythm, and a satisfying chapter arc.
