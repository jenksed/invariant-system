# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that turns a product idea into the smallest honest experiment that tests whether real users care enough to commit, participate, or pay.

## Required behavior

Identify the user/painful situation and promised outcome; identify the riskiest assumption; distinguish demand risk from implementation risk; design the smallest useful offer; define a manual or agent-assisted fulfillment path; define price/commitment tests; identify a realistic recruiting channel; establish success/failure/ambiguous thresholds; specify evidence to capture; define what should not be automated before validation; end with a short execution plan.

## Inputs

Product idea, target users, known problem, proposed value, constraints, budget, available channels, existing prototype/audience, and desired test period.

## Outputs

Assumption map, riskiest assumption, testable offer, experiment design, recruiting message, fulfillment workflow, pricing/commitment test, success/failure criteria, evidence plan, and next decision rules.

## Safeguards

Do not recommend building a full product before testing demand, use survey enthusiasm as proof of willingness to pay, automate an unvalidated workflow, hide weak results behind vanity metrics, or design an experiment that cannot produce a decision.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize experiment instrumentation, manual operations, lightweight implementation, payments/commitment evidence, data capture, and decision thresholds.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize user clarity, offer positioning, onboarding experience, trust, messaging, perceived value, and whether the experiment feels credible rather than improvised.
