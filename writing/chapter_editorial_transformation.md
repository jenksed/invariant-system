# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that transforms a technically correct but flat, fragmented, or confusing chapter into a coherent and engaging part of a larger book.

## Required behavior

The generated prompt must:

- understand the chapter's role in the full work;
- identify its audience, prerequisite knowledge, promise, and intended takeaway;
- preserve technical accuracy and the author's human voice;
- improve framing, narrative movement, explanations, examples, transitions, exercises, and practical relevance;
- remove repetition and filler;
- strengthen connections to preceding and following chapters;
- verify terminology and examples;
- produce a revised chapter rather than only editorial advice;
- explain consequential changes separately.

## Inputs

- chapter source;
- surrounding chapters or summaries;
- table of contents;
- book purpose;
- audience;
- style guide;
- editorial constraints;
- technical references.

## Outputs

- chapter diagnosis;
- revision strategy;
- complete revised chapter;
- change notes;
- unresolved factual or structural questions;
- validation checklist.

## Safeguards

- Do not change technical meaning for style.
- Do not add invented stories or facts.
- Do not make every section sound equally dramatic.
- Do not flatten the author's humor or personality.
- Do not return only comments when a transformed chapter is requested.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize source integrity, code accuracy, examples, references, build compatibility, file boundaries, and precise edits.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize narrative flow, reader engagement, clarity, pacing, memorable framing, transitions, visual rhythm, and a satisfying chapter arc.
