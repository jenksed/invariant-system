# Shape Long-Form Writing From Fragments

Use when raw fragments and/or a beat map exist and the task is to turn them into coherent prose without losing the original substance.

Work progressively rather than generating the entire piece from a vague summary.

For each section/beat:

1. identify the job it must do for the reader;
2. select the strongest available fragments/evidence;
3. choose the form that best carries them (argument, scene, example, explanation, list, contrast, etc.);
4. draft that unit;
5. check continuity with what came before and what it must enable next;
6. preserve factual/evidence boundaries and the user's authentic voice;
7. revise structure before polishing sentences when the unit is not doing its job.

Do not manufacture connective filler just to satisfy an outline. If a beat lacks substance, return to fragment mining or research.
