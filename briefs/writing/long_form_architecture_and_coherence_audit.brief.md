# Prompt Generation Brief

## Purpose

Create a reusable prompt that audits an entire book, handbook, curriculum, or long report as one connected reader experience.

## Required behavior

Identify audience, promise, and intended transformation; map reader journey and purpose of major sections; inspect prerequisite order; find repetition, contradictions, terminology drift, missing bridges, abrupt transitions, pacing problems, and unfulfilled promises; evaluate examples/exercises/summaries/callbacks; inspect TOC/navigation; distinguish local prose issues from structural problems; recommend a prioritized revision sequence; preserve strong material rather than rewriting indiscriminately.

## Inputs

Complete manuscript or repository, table of contents, audience, purpose, style guide, known concerns, reference works, and rendered output when available.

## Outputs

Executive assessment, reader-journey map, section-purpose map, structural defects, repetition/gap analysis, terminology/continuity findings, prioritized revision plan, keep/move/merge/expand/tighten/rebuild recommendations, and validation criteria.

## Safeguards

Do not perform only line editing, judge chapters in isolation, remove deliberate repetition without checking pedagogical value, rewrite the author's voice into generic prose, or call a technically complete manuscript coherent without reader-facing review.

## Sol optimization

Emphasize repository structure, source files, build system, cross-references, navigation, generated output, validation commands, and exact revision locations.

## Fable optimization

Emphasize reader orientation, narrative motion, conceptual progression, pacing, voice, examples, visual hierarchy, transitions, and whether the work is genuinely compelling to read.
