## Claim boundary

This receipt establishes only the behavior actually reconstructed and observed against the named local runtime.

It does not establish that:

- the target cloud has identical authorization, timing, quota, regional, billing, control-plane, or managed-service behavior;
- the emulator caused the original remote incident;
- a local fix is sufficient for production acceptance.

Any such claim needs separate evidence.
